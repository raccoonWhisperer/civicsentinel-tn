Terms of use

Last updated 6 August 2026

The short version

  • Civic Sentinel is an independent community project. It is not the county, not any government agency, and has no authority to make anyone fix anything.
  • Filing here does not report anything to the government, and it is never the way to report an emergency.
  • What you file becomes a permanent public record. Say true things and stand behind them.
  • An issue's status describes what this record shows — not a promise that any official has acted.

1. What this site is

Civic Sentinel is a public log of community issues in Rutherford County, Tennessee, maintained by residents. It lets anyone file a concern, gives it a tracking ID, and follows it on an append-only timeline that cannot be quietly rewritten.

2. What this site is not

  • Not a government service. We are not affiliated with Rutherford County, the Board of Education, TDEC, or any other agency. Nothing filed here reaches them.
  • Not an emergency service. If there is danger to life or property, call 911. A sinkhole opening under a road is a 911 matter, not a form-filling matter.
  • Not legal or engineering advice. Nothing here substitutes for a lawyer, a licensed engineer, or a geologist.
  • Not a party to any litigation. This site takes no position on any pending case and is not evidence of anything.

Filing here does not notify any official. If you need the county to act, contact the county — the "Take action" section lists who to reach. Use this site to make sure the concern is on a record that cannot be edited away.

3. Filing a report

By submitting, you confirm that:

  • What you are describing is true to the best of your knowledge.
  • You are describing a real condition, not a grievance about a named individual.
  • You have the right to publish any photo you attach, and it does not expose someone else's private information.
  • You accept that your report, its location and its photo become public and permanent.

You grant us permission to publish and keep what you submit as part of the public record. You keep ownership of your own words and images.

4. What you may not file

  • Anything false, or filed to harass, intimidate or defame a person.
  • Personal information about other people — names, addresses, phone numbers, faces — where they have not agreed to it.
  • Campaign material, candidate promotion, or fundraising of any kind.
  • Anything unlawful, threatening, or that endangers someone.
  • Automated or bulk submissions.

Reports are reviewed before publication and we may decline to publish. Declining is not a judgement about whether your concern is real.

5. Statuses mean what this record shows

This is the most important thing on the page, so it gets its own section.

An issue marked "Acknowledged" means this project received and logged it. It does not mean a county official saw it, accepted it, or agreed to do anything. Where an entry does describe official action, it cites the public document it came from. Where there is no citation, no official action is being claimed.

The same applies to reference data. Map points, well logs and boundaries are shown for context and are approximate. Always confirm against the official source record before relying on any of it.

6. The record is append-only

Published issues are not deleted or edited. Corrections are made by adding to the timeline. We will remove content that is unlawful, dangerous, or that exposes a third party's personal information — write to us and explain. Your own contact details are private and can be erased at any time; see the Privacy page.

The site is provided as-is. We do not guarantee it will be available, that every entry is accurate, or that any issue will be resolved. Reports are written by residents, and we do not independently verify each one.
