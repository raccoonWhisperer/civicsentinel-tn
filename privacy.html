Privacy

Last updated 6 August 2026

The short version

  • What you write in a report becomes public. The category, summary, description, map pin, address and any photo are published on the permanent record.
  • Your name and contact details do not. They are stored so we can tell you when your issue moves, and they are excluded from the public record at the database level — not just hidden in the page.
  • We never store your raw IP address. Only a salted one-way hash, used to stop one source flooding the log.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics trackers. No Google Analytics, no ad pixels, no cross-site profiling.
  • The record is append-only. Once an issue is published it is not edited away or deleted — including at your request. Please read the section on that before you file.

When you subscribe to updates or meeting reminders

We store your email address and which list you asked for. Nothing else. It is never published, never sold, and never shared with a campaign, a candidate, or any third party. You can ask us to remove it at any time.

When you simply read the site

We do not set analytics cookies and we do not run advertising or social tracking pixels. We do not build a profile of you. Your browser does, however, contact the third parties listed below in order to render the page.

The record is permanent — please read this before you file

Civic Sentinel exists because a record that can be quietly edited is not a record. Every issue carries an append-only timeline, and the database physically refuses updates and deletions to that timeline. Its integrity is the whole product.

The practical consequence for you:

  • A published report is not taken down because you later regret filing it.
  • Corrections are made by adding an entry to the timeline, not by rewriting what is already there.
  • Reports are reviewed by a moderator before publication, so nothing appears instantly — but once it appears, treat it as permanent.

We will remove content that is unlawful, that endangers someone, or that exposes personal information about a third party who did not consent. Write to us and explain; we would rather fix a genuine harm than defend a policy.

Your own contact details are a separate matter — those are private, and you can have them erased at any time without touching the public record.

Who can see the private fields

Reporter names and contact details are readable only by the site's operators, through an authenticated console. The public interface has no permission to read those columns at all: the database rejects the request rather than relying on the page not to display them. Photos are the exception — they live in public storage and anyone with the URL can view them.

How long we keep things

  • Published issues and their timelines: indefinitely. That is the point.
  • Reporter contact details: kept while the issue is open so we can update you, and erased on request.
  • Subscriber emails: until you unsubscribe.
  • Rate-limit hashes: short-lived operational data, pruned periodically.

Children

This site is meant for adults participating in local civic matters. It is not directed at children, and we ask that people under 13 not submit contact information.

Your requests

Write to contact@civicsentinel-tn.com to see what we hold about you, correct it, erase your contact details, or unsubscribe. Please include your tracking ID if your request concerns a report.

Changes to this page

If we change how the site handles information, we update this page and change the date at the top.
